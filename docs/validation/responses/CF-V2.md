# CF-V2 · Product validation: The producer: would you trust being paid this way?

- **Issue:** #11
- **Country / general region:** Nigeria
- **Did you run the demo locally?** no

## 1. First read — what do you think it is?

*Read the README only up to its first `##` heading. Write this before reading anything else.*

- What does MicoPay Café do, in your own words?
  > I thought MicoPay Café was a digital payment platform that helps coffee buyers pay farmers based on the weight and quality of their produce, reducing payment delays and disputes.
- Who is it for?
  > Coffee buyers and cooperatives paying farmers who bring in their harvest.
- What problem does it solve?
  > It aims to make payment at delivery quicker and clearer, so farmers can see how the weight, price and quality deductions determine what they are paid.

## 2. After the full README — what did you get wrong?

- What did you misunderstand in step 1, or what surprised you?
  > My first impression was broadly right. I was surprised by the detailed digital receipts, payment verification and protection against duplicate payments.
- What is still unclear?
  > How farmers without smartphones or reliable internet would access their money, especially while the MicoPay app connection is still planned.

## 3. Producers and middlemen where you live

- Your country / general region (no city or address needed).
  > Nigeria.
- Where you live, do local producers (coffee, fruit, vegetables, milk, fish…) sell through middlemen? Who buys from them, and who sells to the final buyer?
  > In Nigeria, many small-scale farmers sell their produce through local buyers and middlemen.
- How and when are they paid? (cash on the spot, days or weeks later, bank transfer, an app…)
  > From what I have observed, payment is often made through cash or bank transfers.
- Do you live this yourself, see it up close (family, neighbours, work), or only hear about it?
  > This is based on what I have observed where I live.
- What is the biggest problem you notice for the producer? (price, late payment, no record of how the price was computed, carrying cash, safety…)
  > Farmers may face inaccurate weighing, price disagreements and unclear deductions. Reliable internet access and digital literacy could also affect adoption in rural communities.

## 4. Value

- **How useful does it look to you?** 1 (not at all) – 5 (very). Why?
  > 3/5. A system that records weight, price and deductions transparently could help farmers understand their payments and reduce disputes. I am unsure how well it would work where internet access or digital literacy is limited.
- What is the most valuable part?
  > Recording the weight, price and deductions transparently so the farmer can understand the payment.
- What is missing?
  > A practical way to use the money without a smartphone or reliable internet, clearer explanations of deductions, and clear payment notifications when the network fails.
- What would stop you (or the person in your issue's role) from using it?
  > Unreliable internet access and difficulty using a digital service could make it hard for farmers to receive or access their money.

## 5. The MicoPay app

*The README section "How it connects to the MicoPay app (planned)" describes producers getting paid in the MicoPay app and cashing out with an agent, who could be the buyer itself.*

- Does that connection make the product more useful to you? What would it change for you?
  > It could make the product more useful if farmers can access their payment through an agent, but the app connection is still planned. An option that works without a smartphone or reliable internet would matter too.

## 6. What confused you

- Which screen, step or term confused you, and what did you expect instead?
  > It is still unclear how farmers without smartphones or reliable internet would access their money. I would also like each deduction on the receipt explained in simple language.

## 7. Questions for your issue

*Copy the questions from your issue here and answer them.*

- If you were paid for your delivery this way, would you trust it? What would make you trust it more?
  > I would want to see a reliable payment confirmation and a clear receipt before relying on it. The README's payment verification and duplicate-payment protection are reassuring, but the prototype is on Testnet and the MicoPay app connection is still planned. Clear status notifications and an accessible way to cash out would make me more confident.
- Read the receipt described in the README (kilos, price, deductions): could you tell how much you were paid and why?
  > The kilos, price and deductions make the calculation more understandable, but I would want each deduction explained in simple language to understand exactly why it was applied.
- What would you need to actually use that money (phone, signal, cashing out)?
  > I would need a way to receive and access the payment despite unreliable phone or internet access, such as a USSD or agent-assisted option, and a way to cash out.
